# PMO Status Reporting Guidelines
## F3 Status Report Generator – Knowledge Base

### 1. Purpose
These guidelines define how project status reports must be structured, worded, and classified
for Engineering & Construction Services projects. All F3 reports must align with these standards.

---

### 2. Overall Status (RAG Classification)

| Status | Color | Definition |
|--------|-------|------------|
| **GREEN** | 🟢 | Project is on schedule, within budget, and no critical issues are blocking progress. Minor risks are being managed. |
| **YELLOW** | 🟡 | Project has one or more at-risk items that could affect the schedule or budget if not addressed. Active mitigation is in progress. |
| **RED** | 🔴 | Project has a critical issue causing or likely to cause significant schedule delay, budget overrun, or scope impact. Escalation required. |

**Classification Rules:**
- If any milestone is delayed >5 business days → minimum YELLOW
- If budget variance exceeds 10% → minimum YELLOW
- If a critical-path item is blocked with no mitigation → RED
- If two or more at-risk items co-exist without mitigation → RED

---

### 3. Report Sections (All Audiences)

Every F3 report must include these five sections in this order:

1. **Header** – Project name, reporting period, prepared by, report date, overall status
2. **Progress This Period** – What was accomplished during the reporting period
3. **Upcoming Milestones** – What is planned for the next 2–3 weeks
4. **Items Requiring Your Attention** – Risks, blockers, decisions needed
5. **Notes** – Any additional context, assumptions, or CONFIRM markers

---

### 4. Audience-Specific Standards

#### 4.1 Executive Reports
- **Word limit:** ≤ 300 words total
- **Tone:** High-level, strategic, decision-focused
- **Must include:** Overall Status (RAG), top 2 risks, key decisions made/pending
- **Avoid:** Technical detail, operational minutiae, raw data tables
- **Format:** Brief paragraphs, no more than 3–4 bullets per section

#### 4.2 Client Reports
- **Tone:** Professional, non-technical, reassuring, mitigation-focused
- **Must include:** Schedule progress, upcoming visible milestones, risk impact in plain language
- **Must avoid:** Internal team conflicts, resourcing issues, unverified speculation, internal budget details
- **Format:** Plain language a non-PM could understand; no jargon

#### 4.3 Site Team Reports
- **Tone:** Operational, direct, data-heavy
- **Must include:** Concrete dates, % completion per task, dependencies, blockers, next actions
- **Format:** Structured bullets with specifics (e.g., "Structural steel: 90% complete, target 18 Feb, At Risk")
- **Avoid:** Executive-level narrative; site team needs facts and actions, not summaries

---

### 5. CONFIRM Placeholder Rules

Use `[CONFIRM]` wherever:
- A date, percentage, or figure is missing from inputs
- Two sources give conflicting information (surface both with CONFIRM)
- An action or decision has not been explicitly confirmed in inputs
- Status cannot be determined from available data

**Never invent:** dates, budget figures, percentage completion, decision outcomes, or risk resolutions.

---

### 6. Prohibited Content (All Reports)
- Legal, contractual, or financial advice
- HR-related recommendations (hiring, firing, performance judgments)
- Information not present in the structured inputs or this knowledge base
- Speculation presented as fact
- Internal confidential details in Client-facing reports

---

### 7. Schedule Status Definitions

| Status | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **On Track** | Task is progressing as planned; no risk to completion date |
| **At Risk** | Task may miss its target date; active monitoring required |
| **Delayed** | Task has already missed its planned completion date |
| **Complete** | Task is 100% done and accepted |

---

### 8. Report Quality Checklist

Before any report is finalized, verify:
- [ ] Overall Status RAG is present and justified
- [ ] All five sections are present and correctly labeled
- [ ] No invented facts (all uncertain data shows CONFIRM)
- [ ] Audience tone is appropriate (Executive = brief; Client = plain; Site = detailed)
- [ ] Client report contains no internal-only information
- [ ] Budget and schedule data align with the schedule CSV (if provided)
